Three Ways to Dry

Three frames from one minute on the branch, nothing stacked: each panel is its own exposure, cut to the same box around the perch. The wings never came down. What changed was the head, which is how a cormorant watches the water while it waits to be dry. The middle frame is Drying Wings on its own page.
