Jeanie Johnston

A replica, and the original is the reason anyone built one. The Jeanie Johnston carried emigrants out of Tralee through the worst of the Famine years and lost not a single passenger or crew member across sixteen crossings — which was so far from ordinary on that route that it is the whole of her fame. The ships she sailed among are remembered for the opposite.
