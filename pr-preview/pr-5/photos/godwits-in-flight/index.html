Godwits in Flight

They had stood in the shallows for most of the morning, in a line the tide drew, and went up all together at eighteen minutes past eleven. Black-tailed, by the white wing-bars and the legs trailing past the tail. The first outing with a longer lens; on the old one this would have been a stripe along the far shore.
