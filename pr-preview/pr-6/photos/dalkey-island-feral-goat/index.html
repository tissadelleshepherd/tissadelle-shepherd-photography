Feral Goat

There is a fence on this part of the island, and a warden was on the ground the day this was taken — but the fence is there to keep people out rather than goats in, and the animal is loose. The herd is feral: domestic stock, generations back, living untended. That ancestry alone keeps the frame out of the wildlife records, which exclude feral animals outright however long they have been at large. Free to roam, and ineligible for its parentage.
