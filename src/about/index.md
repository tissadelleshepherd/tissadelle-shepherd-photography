<h1 class="page-title">About this archive</h1>
<p class="page-intro">
  <strong>Unofficial fan work.</strong> This is a fanfiction site: a fan's reframing of
  Dermot R. Cochran's photography portfolio as an in-story archive from his novel
  <em><a href="https://fianilchruinne.com">Fian Ilchruinne</a></em>. It is not by the author of
  the novel, not endorsed by him, and asserts nothing about the story.
</p>

<h2>In the story</h2>
<p>
  The conceit is that these are the photographs Tissadelle Shepherd keeps: a Star Ranger's own
  frames of the places and creatures she stopped for. She is the photographer and never the
  subject, so no picture here shows her, and none is captioned as anyone in the story.
</p>
<p>
  The record says she reads instruments well and does not claim past her evidence. An archive
  of hers would be like this one: a frame that withholds, and a note that says only what was
  there. The places and notes on each page are the photographs' own, as their real
  photographer filed them, except where a note named something of our own century (a date, a
  competition, a present-day agency): those phrases are reworded, and the dates are left off.
  Photographs showing something unmistakably of our own century are kept out of the archive's
  lists.
</p>
<p>
  Six centuries lie between our time and hers, and the archive does not pretend otherwise.
  The places keep their old names. What else they kept, the frames do not say.
</p>
<p>
  The <a href="/plates/">Plates from the record</a> are different: generated illustrations of
  the novel's worlds, reproduced unchanged from fianilchruinne.com and labelled as such. They
  are not photographs, not hers, and none shows a person.
</p>
